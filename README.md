Our project is a Mock-Streaming Service, similar to that of Netflix or Hulu. 

On the site, you're greated with a home page that includes a list of reccomended, favorites, and movies collection. At the top is a hero with a featured movie, which lists its title, description, runtime, year, lead, and genre.

The browse page is split into 3 different categories based on sorting - by clicking the Browse button in the nav bar, you are sent to Browse A-Z, which sorts all available movies in a list A-Z. By clicking the dropdown for sort, you can switch to the different HTML pages. Genre sorts the movies in different sections like Netflix does, allowing users to have better visual seperation for movies of different sections. Meanwhile, Year will sort movies from newest to oldest, allowing the user to find movies that recently came out, or classics they want to rewatch. At the top of all 3 of those browse pages, there is a filter to search for genre, year, and lead in each of the sorted ways. For ex.
on the Genre sort searching "Ryan Reynolds" as lead will give you 2 movies in their specific genre sections. The many methods of sorting is the solution to finding a good movie with the right specifications!

The author page lists all the information about us, the team. It includes the course name, our names, our emails, our pictures, and a short bio for each of us.

The FAQ page contains 8 distinct, fequently-asked questions, with answers/solutions to each. It will use a bootstrap accordion component for each set of questions and answers.
